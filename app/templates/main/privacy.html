Who we are

The data controller is {{ LIBRARY_NAME }}. Our contact for anything about your data is the librarian: {{ librarian_email }}.

What we collect

  • Your name, address, email address and phone number.
  • Records of the books you borrow and return (your loan records), and books you ask for.
  • The date and time you agreed to this notice.

Why we collect it

To run the library: to know who has which book, to send you reminders when a book is due or overdue, to tell you when a book you asked for is back, and to contact you about your membership. We do not use your details for anything else, and we never sell or share them.

When you ask for a book that someone else has, we email them to say "someone is looking for this book". We never tell them who you are, and we never tell you who has the book.

How long we keep it

  • Loan history is kept for 2 years, then deleted automatically.
  • Applications that are not approved are deleted after 30 days.
  • If you close your account, your personal details are deleted. Past loan records are kept for up to 2 years but are no longer linked to you (they show as "former member").

Your rights

You can see and change your details at any time under "My details", and you can close your account there. You can also ask the librarian for a copy of your data, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted. You have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).
